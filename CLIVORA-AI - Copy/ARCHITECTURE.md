### 1. Document Overview & System Identity

**CLINOVA AI** is an explainable, multimodal, human-in-the-loop healthcare triage assistant engineered for Indian public health infrastructure, including:
- Primary Health Centers (PHCs)
- Community Health Centers (CHCs) and Sub-Divisional Hospitals
- Government District Hospital Outpatient Departments (OPD)
- Public Health Outreach and Mobile Camps
- Institutional and Campus Health Clinics

Operating in high-volume, resource-constrained environments, the system automates the preliminary intake and organization of patient information. By combining vernacular voice transcription, text entry, and lab report optical character recognition (OCR), CLINOVA AI structures messy intake narratives into standardized clinical summaries (SOAP format: Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) alongside prioritized urgency classifications.

## 2. Clinical Goals & Non-Goals

### 2.1 Clinical & Technical Goals
- **Reduce Frontline Triage Wait Times:** Provide preliminary risk stratification within seconds of patient intake to identify critically ill patients in crowded waiting areas.
- **Overcome Vernacular Language Barriers:** Enable frontline healthcare workers and patients to speak naturally in regional Indian languages (Odia, Hindi, and Indian English).
- **Ensure Complete Explainability:** Every urgency flag and risk classification must be bound to a deterministic, traceable rule code (`TRIAGE-R01` to `TRIAGE-R06`) rather than an opaque, probabilistic confidence score.
- **Enforce Human Clinical Oversight:** The system acts strictly as an informational assistant; no AI classification reaches a permanent health chart without attending clinician validation.
- **Privacy by Design:** Strip direct personal identifiers (Indian phone numbers, email addresses, 12-digit Aadhaar/government IDs) before persistence or generative inference.
- **Zero-Cloud Offline Reliability:** Operate seamlessly in rural, air-gapped settings with local deterministic fallbacks (`DEMO_MODE=true`) when external API keys or cellular connectivity are absent.

### 2.2 Explicit System Non-Goals
To prevent clinical harm and regulatory overreach, CLINOVA AI is explicitly **NOT** designed to:
- **Make Autonomous Diagnoses:** The system performs syndromic triage categorization, never assigning an autonomous disease diagnosis.
- **Prescribe Medications:** The system does not recommend specific pharmaceutical dosages, drug schedules, or dispense orders.
- **Discharge Patients Automatically:** Low-acuity or routine classifications still require attending staff verification before physical discharge.
- **Operate Without Human Oversight:** No AI output progresses to an official electronic health record (EHR) or referral slip without explicit clinician approval.

## 7. Subsystem Architectural Specifications

### 7.1 Multimodal Intake Pipeline
The intake subsystem (`frontend/src/app/intake/page.tsx` and `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/intake.py`) guides patients or kiosk operators through a 4-step wizard:
1. **Consent & Context:** Collects age, biological sex, presenting facility tier, encounter type, and preferred language.
2. **Symptoms & Speech:** Features the 6-state `<VoiceRecorder />` widget (`idle` $\rightarrow$ `requesting` $\rightarrow$ `recording` $\rightarrow$ `processing` $\rightarrow$ `success` $\rightarrow$ `error`) with native audio playback for Odia, Hindi, and English.
3. **Medical Report OCR:** Features `<ReportUploader />` allowing lab report image uploads and displaying extracted CBC fields with manual verification checkboxes.
4. **Review & Dispatch:** Submits the consolidated intake payload to `POST /api/v1/cases/`.

### 7.2 Deterministic Risk Signal Engine (`TRIAGE-R01` - `R06`)
Located in `backend/app/services/risk_engine.py`, this engine guarantees explainability and patient safety:
- **`TRIAGE-R01` (URGENT REVIEW):** Acute Respiratory Distress (Keywords: `shortness of breath`, `dyspnea`, `wheezing`, `saans lene mein takleef`, `dam phula`).
- **`TRIAGE-R02` (URGENT REVIEW):** Severe Hemorrhage & Active Bleeding (Keywords: `severe bleeding`, `hemoptysis`, `blood in stool`, `khun nikalna`, `rakta srava`).
- **`TRIAGE-R03` (URGENT REVIEW):** Altered Consciousness & Neurological Urgency (Keywords: `unconscious`, `syncope`, `seizure`, `behoshi`, `chetana hariba`).
- **`TRIAGE-R04` (URGENT REVIEW):** Acute Chest Pain & Cardiovascular Urgency (Keywords: `crushing chest pressure`, `radiating to left arm`, `seene mein dard`, `chhati re jantrana`).
- **`TRIAGE-R05` (PRIORITY):** High Sustained Fever & Infectious Outbreak Signal (Keywords: `fever for 3 days`, `chills`, `tez bukhar`, `prabal jwara`).
- **`TRIAGE-R06` (PRIORITY):** Severe Acute Abdominal Pain (Keywords: `severe abdominal pain`, `pet mein tez dard`, `peta re asahya jantrana`).
- **Default (ROUTINE):** Standard non-emergent outpatient presentation.

### 7.3 Multilingual Normalization Engine
Located in `backend/app/services/translation_service.py` and `speech_service.py`:
- **Dual Representation Principle:** Raw vernacular transcripts are immutably stored in `raw_symptoms` and `speech_transcript`. The generated English summary is presented side-by-side, ensuring attending physicians can cross-verify what was actually spoken.
- **Disfluency Cleaning:** Strips conversational hesitation tokens while strictly preserving clinical semantic markers.

### 7.4 Medical Report OCR Parameter Parsing
Located in `backend/app/services/ocr_service.py`:
- Parses Complete Blood Count (CBC) parameters: Hemoglobin (Hb), Total Leukocyte Count (WBC), Platelet Count, and Red Blood Cell Count (RBC).
- Generates bounding box coordinates `[ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax]` for visual auditing.
- Reports field-level confidence percentages and enforces `verification_status: "pending"` until a clinician manually inspects the values.

### 7.5 Human-in-the-Loop Review Gate & Clinician Workflow
Located in `frontend/src/app/review/case/[caseId]/page.tsx` and `backend/app/api/v1/endpoints/review.py`:
- Accessible exclusively by users with the role of `doctor` or `nurse`.
- Medical officers execute one of 4 decisive actions:
  1. `APPROVE`: Formally stamps the case as clinically approved with doctor name and UTC timestamp.
  2. `EDIT`: Allows the clinician to directly edit the clinical summary, adjust the urgency level, or append notes.
  3. `REJECT`: Marks intake as unusable/invalid, flagging the patient for immediate direct physician assessment.
  4. `ESCALATE`: Elevates the case for secondary/tertiary hospital transfer and compiles a referral support note.

### 7.6 Referral Note Synthesis & Printing Pipeline
Located in `frontend/src/app/review/case/[caseId]/referral/page.tsx`:
- Generates a standardized clinical transfer document containing: originating facility, synthetic case ID, chronological symptom trajectory, extracted lab values, triggered risk signals, receiving emergency instructions, and doctor signature line.
- Embedded `@media print` CSS formats the document cleanly for physical paper printing or one-click browser PDF export.

### 7.7 In-Flight PII Anonymization & Privacy Layer
Located in `backend/app/services/anonymizer.py`:
- **Scrubbing Specifications:**
  - Phone numbers (Indian 10-digit mobile formats) $\rightarrow$ `[PHONE_REMOVED]`
  - Email addresses $\rightarrow$ `[EMAIL_REDACTED]`
  - Indian National Government IDs (12-digit Aadhaar formats) $\rightarrow$ `[GOVT_ID_REDACTED]`
- **Synthetic Identifiers:** Generates random, non-identifying case tokens (`CLV-DEMO-xxx`).

### 7.8 Medicolegal Audit Logging Architecture
Located in `backend/app/services/audit.py` and `backend/app/models/audit.py`:
- Records immutable PostgreSQL audit entries capturing:
  - `timestamp`: UTC ISO 8601 timestamp
  - `user_id` & `user_email`: Electronic identifier of the acting clinician
  - `action`: Standardized event code (`CASE_INTAKE_CREATED`, `REVIEW_ACTION_APPROVE`, etc.)
  - `resource_type` & `resource_id`: Target entity and synthetic case identifier
  - `ip_address` & `user_agent`: Network audit trail
  - `details`: Human-readable summary of the exact modification or action performed.

## 8. Architectural Roadmap & Planned Capabilities

The following capabilities are specified in project blueprints for future development cycles:

1. **Streaming Audio Ingestion WebSocket (`/api/v1/intake/ws/speech`):**
   - Live bidirectional streaming protocol for chunked audio processing and real-time transcription feedback on low-bandwidth connections.
2. **On-Premise Neural Speech & Translation Containers:**
   - Dedicated local OpenAI Whisper or Bhashini STT container runtime for air-gapped rural centers.
   - Dedicated open-weight IndicTrans2 translation models supporting 22 scheduled Indian languages.
3. **Complex Prescription Vision Pipeline:**
   - Multi-page document handling and handwriting vision fine-tuning using PaddleOCR and Tesseract to digitize handwritten OPD doctor slips.
4. **Offline CRDT Database Synchronization:**

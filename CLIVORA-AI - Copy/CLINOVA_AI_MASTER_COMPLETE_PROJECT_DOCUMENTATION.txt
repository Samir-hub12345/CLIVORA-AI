SECTION 1: PROJECT VISION, CLINICAL PURPOSE & BRANDING EVOLUTION
================================================================================

1.1 The Healthcare Challenge Clinova AI Solves
Across primary healthcare centers, public district hospitals, emergency casualty
departments, and rural community clinics worldwide, clinical teams face severe
structural challenges:
1. Overwhelming Patient Volumes: Emergency departments and outpatient clinics
   experience severe overcrowding, leading to critical delays in identifying
   time-sensitive emergencies.
2. Clinical Burnout & Documentation Burden: Clinicians spend up to 40% of their
   working shifts entering medical notes, vitals, and structured documentation
   instead of examining patients.
3. Rural Specialist Scarcity: Remote health centers frequently operate without
   on-site senior specialists or cardiologists, forcing junior staff or nursing
   officers to make complex triage determinations with limited support.
4. Multimodal & Multi-Lingual Barriers: Patients present symptoms verbally in
   regional languages, carry physical paper pathology reports (such as Complete
   Blood Count laboratory printouts), and describe symptoms with varying levels
   of health literacy.

Clinova AI was conceived and built specifically to resolve these bottlenecks by
providing a unified, multimodal, role-separated clinical triage and documentation
platform that automates intake, standardizes clinical summaries, highlights red
flags deterministically, and prepares ready-to-sign SOAP notes for doctors.

1.2 Core Clinical Philosophy & Non-Diagnostic Decision Support
A foundational principle embedded in every layer of Clinova AI is:
"CLINOVA AI NEVER DIAGNOSES PATIENTS."

In medical informatics, autonomous AI diagnostic claims create catastrophic
liability, risk algorithmic hallucination, and violate healthcare regulations.
Instead, Clinova AI operates strictly as a Clinical Decision Support (CDS) tool.
- It normalizes messy patient descriptions into medical terminology.
- It extracts structured laboratory parameters from uploaded photos or scans.
- It flags physiological red flags (e.g., tachycardia, hypoxemia, hypotension).
- It synthesizes structured SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) drafts.
- It presents everything to a human physician on a single screen for review.
The attending physician remains the sole decision-maker who approves, edits, or
overrides any triage category or clinical recommendation.

1.3 Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Medical Mandate
The platform implements an enforced 4-step Human-in-the-Loop workflow:
1. Patient or Front-Desk Staff initiates intake (voice, text, documents, vitals).
2. Clinova AI analyzes multimodal inputs, anonymizes personal data, evaluates
   deterministic safety rules, and drafts a clinical brief.
3. Triage Nurse / Staff reviews baseline vitals, confirms intake completeness,
   and routes the patient to an attending doctor or specific clinical specialty.
4. Licensed Physician reviews the patient chart, verified vitals, OCR data,
   and synthesized draft, then applies digital sign-off, records the medical plan,
   and issues a formal referral note if secondary escalation is required.

1.4 Branding Unification: Clivora AI to Clinova AI Migration
Originally titled "Clivora AI" in early exploratory prototypes, the platform
underwent a comprehensive audit and rebranding migration to "Clinova AI".
- All user-facing interfaces, browser `<title>` tags, headers, footers, demo
  cards, and email domains were unified under "Clinova AI" (e.g., doctor@clinova.ai).
- Stray experimental wording (such as "Before AI use only continue") was audited
  and replaced with clear, transparent clinical explanations.
- Underlying database names and technical service containers maintained backward
  compatibility to guarantee zero data loss and avoid breaking database volumes.

1.5 Target User Roles & Clinical Environments
Clinova AI provides specialized, tailored user experiences for four key personas:
1. Patient / Family Member: Accessible intake, voice-first interaction, regional
   language support, real-time case tracking, and emergency facility numbers.
2. Triage Staff / Nurse: Rapid patient queue, front-desk vitals capture modal,
   acuity classification, room assignment, and doctor routing.
3. Attending Physician / Doctor: One-Screen Review Workspace, side-by-side
   intake narrative and vitals, AI SOAP note editor, one-click case sign-off,
   and clinical referral note export.
4. Facility Administrator / CMO: Hospital-wide telemetry, user account access
   controls, AI engine health monitoring, and tamper-evident audit logging.

2.2 Microservices & Docker Orchestration
The system is managed via Docker Compose (`docker-compose.yml`) containing 4
tightly integrated services communicating over a dedicated internal network:
1. `clinova-frontend`: Builds from `./frontend/Dockerfile`, exposes port 3000.
2. `clinova-backend`: Builds from `./backend/Dockerfile`, exposes port 8000.
3. `clinova-db`: Uses official image `postgres:16-alpine`, exposes port 5432.
4. `clinova-redis`: Uses official image `redis:7-alpine`, exposes port 6379.

2.3 Frontend Stack
- Framework: Next.js 14.2.35 with modern App Router (`/src/app`).
- UI Library: React 18 with React Server Components (RSC) and Client Components.
- Language: TypeScript with strict mode enabled (`tsc --noEmit` verified).
- Styling: Tailwind CSS with custom clinical color palettes (Medical Cyan, Slate,
  Emergency Red, Priority Amber, Routine Green).
- Icons: Lucide React icons for high-contrast accessibility.
- State Management: React Hooks (`useState`, `useEffect`, `useContext`) combined
  with LocalStorage persistence for user sessions and network preferences.

2.4 Backend Stack
- Framework: FastAPI (Python 3.12) leveraging Starlette ASGI architecture.
- ORM: SQLAlchemy 2.0 with asynchronous engine (`postgresql+asyncpg`).
- Data Validation: Pydantic v2 schemas enforcing strict request/response shapes.
- Authentication: `python-jose` for JWT encoding/decoding, `passlib[bcrypt]` for
  secure multi-round salted password hashing.
- AI Integration: Official Google GenAI SDK (`google-genai`) interfacing with
  Gemini 1.5 Pro and Gemini 1.5 Flash models with fallback rule simulation.
- PDF & OCR Utilities: `pytesseract`, `Pillow`, `PyPDF2`, `reportlab`.

2.5 Data Persistence Layer (PostgreSQL 16)
- Database Name: `clinova`
- User: `postgres`
- Port: `5432`
- Schema Entities: Users, Patients, Consultations, Triage Cases, Audit Logs.
- Features: Asynchronous connection pooling, automatic foreign key integrity,
  timestamp indexing, and JSONB storage for dynamic clinical vitals and OCR data.

2.6 Caching & Message Queue (Redis 7)
- Service Name: `clinova-redis`
- Port: `6379`
- Purpose: Ephemeral token blacklist, API rate limiting, network status caching,
  and fast queue buffering for pending offline sync cases.

2.7 Networking, Ports & CORS Policies
- Frontend Origin: `http://localhost:3000` (and `http://127.0.0.1:3000`).
- Backend API: `http://localhost:8000` with API prefix `/api/v1`.
- CORS Configuration: Explicitly allows GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE, OPTIONS,
  HEAD from `http://localhost:3000` with preflight credentials validation.

2.8 Environment Configuration & Secrets Management
- Root `.env` and `backend/.env.example` configure:
  `DATABASE_URL`, `REDIS_URL`, `SECRET_KEY`, `ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRE_MINUTES`,
  `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `ENVIRONMENT`, `CORS_ORIGINS`.
- Frontend environment `frontend/.env.local` configures:
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://localhost:8000`.

SECTION 4: ADAPTIVE LOW-BANDWIDTH & REAL-TIME NETWORK RESILIENCE ENGINE
================================================================================

4.1 Problem Statement: Healthcare Delivery in Low-Connectivity Regions
Community clinics, rural primary healthcare sub-centers, and disaster response
camps frequently operate with unstable mobile data (2G / erratic 3G), high packet
loss, or complete internet outages. A healthcare web application that crashes or
hangs indefinitely on a blank loading spinner when a cell tower drops is unusable.

4.2 Network State Machine
Clinova AI implements an automated 4-tier network state machine:
1. `GOOD` (Effective type 4G / broadband, latency < 100ms, downlink > 5 Mbps):
   - Full graphical fidelity, rich SVG animations, instant uncompressed asset
     loading, high-resolution document previews.
2. `NORMAL` (Standard 3G / 4G, latency 100-300ms, downlink 1.5 - 5 Mbps):
   - Standard balanced UI, lazy loading enabled, optimized webp image delivery.
3. `SLOW` (2G / High-latency satellite / congested 3G, latency > 300ms, downlink < 1.5 Mbps):
   - Automatic Low-Bandwidth Mode activates:
   - Disables heavy background gradients, decorative blurs, and animations.
   - Converts image previews to pure textual summaries.
   - Compresses JSON request payloads.
   - Increases API retry timeout budgets with exponential backoff.
   - Displays persistent banner: "Low-Bandwidth Mode Active — High Reliability".
4. `OFFLINE` (Zero connectivity detected):
   - Offline Mode activates:
   - Intercepts intake form submissions and stores them locally in browser
     LocalStorage / IndexedDB with cryptographic UUIDs.
   - Surfaces local queuing confirmation: "Intake Saved Locally. Auto-sync will
     trigger when connection restores."
   - Disables remote network calls and serves cached patient directories.

4.3 Dynamic Network Monitoring & RTT Telemetry
- Implemented in `frontend/src/components/NetworkStatusIndicator.tsx` and custom
  React hooks.
- Continuously listens to `navigator.connection` change events (`change`, `online`,
  `offline`).
- Executes periodic lightweight micro-pings (`HEAD /api/v1/health`) every 15
  seconds to measure true round-trip time (RTT) and jitter rather than relying
  solely on synthetic browser flags.

4.4 Automatic UI/UX Adaptation & Degradation Strategies
- In Slow/Offline mode, document uploads convert from full-file multipart streaming
  to compressed chunks or local file handles.
- Audio recording disables cloud-based heavy neural transcription and falls back
  to local browser speech recognition primitives (`webkitSpeechRecognition`).

4.5 Offline Data Ingestion & Background Sync Queue
- When connectivity transitions from `OFFLINE` -> `SLOW` or `GOOD`:
  1. The network engine fires an automatic `sync` event.
  2. It iterates through the offline storage queue.
  3. Sequentially posts pending cases to `POST /api/v1/cases/submit`.
  4. Displays a non-intrusive toast notification: "3 offline cases synchronized
     with hospital server."

4.6 Manual Low-Bandwidth Override Control
- Recognizing that healthcare staff know their local conditions best, a manual
  toggle switch is placed prominently in the global navigation bar.
- Clinicians can force Low-Bandwidth Mode on at any time, instantly stripping
  decorative overhead and maximizing responsiveness on low-spec hospital hardware.

5.3 Automated SOAP Note Generation
The AI engine parses messy, unstructured multimodal inputs and transforms them

- Objective (O): Physical vitals (BP, HR, SpO2, Temp, RR) with reference flags,

- Assessment (A): Acuity categorization (Resuscitation, Emergent, Urgent, Routine),
  differential considerations for physician evaluation, and identified red flags.
- Plan (P): Immediate bedside nursing recommendations, suggested diagnostic

5.4 Pathology & Lab Report OCR Engine
- Located in `backend/app/services/ocr.py`.

  - Hemoglobin (Hb): Flags values < 10 g/dL (Moderate Anemia), < 7 g/dL (Severe).
  - White Blood Cell Count (WBC): Flags values > 11,000 /µL (Leukocytosis/Infection).
  - Platelet Count: Flags values < 100,000 /µL (Thrombocytopenia/Bleeding risk).
  - Random Blood Sugar (RBS): Flags values > 200 mg/dL or < 70 mg/dL.
- Extracted values include bounding confidence scores and are displayed
  side-by-side with original scanned documents for instant clinician verification.

5.5 Regional Speech-to-Text Pipeline

5.6 Automated PII Anonymization Engine

  - Phone Numbers: Replaced with `[PHONE_REDACTED]`.

SECTION 6: DETERMINISTIC CLINICAL RULE ENGINE & RED-FLAG TRIAGE
================================================================================

6.1 Why Machine Learning Alone Is Insufficient in Emergency Triage
Large Language Models are probabilistic by nature; they can occasionally under-call
an atypical presentation or hallucinate reassuring commentary. In emergency triage,
a failure to identify an acute myocardial infarction or impending airway compromise
is catastrophic.

Therefore, Clinova AI implements a dual-layer safety architecture:
Layer 1: Deterministic Rule Engine (Hardcoded, non-overridable safety rules).
Layer 2: Generative AI Documentation Engine (Assists with synthesis and formatting).
If a patient's presentation triggers any deterministic safety rule, the case is
IMMEDIATELY classified as High Acuity (Urgent / Emergent), irrespective of any
AI output.

6.2 Rule R01: Acute Coronary Syndrome & Ischemic Chest Pain
- Condition: Patient presents with acute chest pain, pressure, tightness, or
  discomfort radiating to the left arm, shoulder, neck, or jaw, accompanied by
  diaphoresis (cold sweating) or dyspnea.
- Triggered Action:
  - Forces Acuity: `URGENT REVIEW` / Level 1-2.
  - Generates Alert Badge: `TRIAGE-R01: ACUTE CORONARY SYNDROME PROTOCOL`.
  - Mandates immediate 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of facility arrival.

6.3 Rule R02: Severe Respiratory Distress & SpO2 Hypoxemia
- Condition: Documented oxygen saturation SpO2 < 90% on room air, OR respiratory
  rate > 30 breaths/min, OR severe audible wheezing with stridor/retractions.
- Triggered Action:
  - Forces Acuity: `RESUSCITATION / EMERGENT`.
  - Generates Alert Badge: `TRIAGE-R02: ACUTE HYPOXEMIC RESPIRATORY FAILURE`.
  - Mandates supplemental oxygen delivery and immediate physician bedside call.

6.4 Rule R03: Acute Stroke / Neurological Deficit (FAST Protocol)
- Condition: Sudden onset facial droop, unilateral arm weakness, slurred speech,
  or acute altered mental status within a known symptom onset window.
- Triggered Action:
  - Forces Acuity: `EMERGENT / STROKE CODE`.
  - Generates Alert Badge: `TRIAGE-R03: ACUTE ISCHEMIC STROKE PROTOCOL`.
  - Initiates immediate non-contrast head CT requisition alert.

6.5 Rule R04: Severe Pediatric Dehydration & Lethargy
- Condition: Child (< 12 years) with history of severe vomiting/diarrhea presenting
  with lethargy, sunken eyes, dry mucous membranes, or capillary refill > 3s.
- Triggered Action:
  - Forces Acuity: `URGENT PEDIATRIC ESCALATION`.
  - Generates Alert Badge: `TRIAGE-R04: SEVERE PEDIATRIC DEHYDRATION ALERT`.

6.6 Rule R05: Severe Sepsis Warning & Hemodynamic Instability
- Condition: Suspected infection combined with two or more SIRS/qSOFA criteria:
  Systolic BP < 90 mmHg, Heart Rate > 100 bpm, Respiratory Rate > 22, Temp > 38.3°C.
- Triggered Action:
  - Forces Acuity: `EMERGENT / SEPSIS PROTOCOL`.
  - Generates Alert Badge: `TRIAGE-R05: HEMODYNAMIC INSTABILITY / SEPSIS ALERT`.

6.7 Rule R06: Routine & Non-Emergent Symptom Routing
- Condition: Patient presents with mild, localized, chronic symptoms (> 7 days)
  with completely normal vital signs and no red flags (e.g., mild skin rash,
  chronic joint ache, minor superficial abrasion).
- Triggered Action:
  - Assigns Acuity: `ROUTINE / NON-URGENT` (ESI Level 4-5).
  - Routes to outpatient clinic queue, preventing emergency room bottlenecking.

6.8 Standard 5-Tier Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Mapping
Clinova AI standardizes all clinical assessments across international triage scales:
- Level 1 (Resuscitation): Immediate life-saving intervention required.
- Level 2 (Emergent): High risk, confused/lethargic, severe pain/distress.
- Level 3 (Urgent): Stable vitals, but requires 2 or more diagnostic resources.
- Level 4 (Less Urgent): Stable, requires 1 simple diagnostic test or prescription.
- Level 5 (Non-Urgent): Stable, routine consultation or dressing change.

SECTION 9: SECURITY, PRIVACY, COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE
================================================================================

9.1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture
RBAC is enforced on the server side using FastAPI dependency injection (`require_roles`):
- `PATIENT`: Can submit intakes, view own profile, and inspect own triage status.
  Blocked from seeing other patients or hospital queues.
- `NURSE / STAFF`: Can view triage queue, record physical vitals, assign doctors,
  and verify intake. Blocked from signing final medical assessments.
- `DOCTOR`: Full clinical authority. Can review charts, edit SOAP notes, modify
  triage acuity, digitally sign cases, and generate referral transfer notes.
- `ADMIN`: Full operational authority. Can manage user accounts, inspect system
  telemetry, and view unredacted forensic audit logs.

9.2 Cryptographic Token Authentication
- Uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with HMAC-SHA256 (`HS256`).
- Standard expiration budget configured via `ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRE_MINUTES`.
- Token payload encapsulates `sub` (User UUID) and `role` (`doctor`, `nurse`, etc.).

9.3 Password Hashing Standards
- Implements Bcrypt cryptographic hashing with randomized per-user salts.
- Plaintext passwords are never stored, never logged in audit files, and never
  transmitted in backend response payloads.

9.4 Immutable Audit Trails & Forensic Event Logging
- Every state-altering action in Clinova AI triggers an automated call to
  `AuditService.log_event`.
- Audit records are stored in a dedicated database table (`audit_logs`) and cannot
  be edited or deleted through application endpoints.

9.5 Non-Diagnostic Legal Framing
- Disclaimers are embedded in the code, database responses, UI landing page,
  intake portal, and referral notes.
- Explicitly declares compliance with medical device software regulations by
  positioning the system solely as administrative and triage decision support.

9.6 HIPAA & Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Alignment
- Ready for integration with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Health Account - ABHA ID) and
  M1/M2/M3 health data standards.
- Designed with strict data isolation, encryption at rest, and audit accountability.

- Issue: Backend container fails to connect to PostgreSQL.
  Resolution: Verify that `clinova-db` is in `healthy` status and that
  `DATABASE_URL=postgresql+asyncpg://postgres:postgres@db:5432/clinova` uses
  the internal Docker service name `db` rather than `localhost`.
- Issue: CORS errors in browser DevTools.
  Resolution: Ensure `CORS_ORIGINS` in `backend/.env` includes `http://localhost:3000`.
